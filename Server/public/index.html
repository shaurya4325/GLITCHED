    // THE STORY
    
      
    The screen cracked. The glitch got out.
    
      
    
        
    It's the night before exams. Shaurya's monitor shatters, a living glitch tears out of it and spreads into the walls, the lights, everything, until the whole room lifts off the ground and he is pulled inside.
    
        
    On the other side are the worlds of the CBSE syllabus, each run by a boss who broke its laws. Fix them with real science: debug Code City, put the laws of motion back, balance the reactions, and heal the body from the inside.
    
        
    Then a portal opens onto the one place every student fears: an exam hall. Waiting at the front is Kanha the Dard, the final exam in person.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    CHAPTER 1 · COMPUTER SCIENCE
    
        
    Code City
    
        
    A neon city where the streets run on logic, and the logic is full of bugs. Trace loops, fix syntax, convert binary and wire logic gates to bring the power back.
    
        
    • Loops & conditions
    • Binary
    • Logic gates
    • Debugging
    
        
    Mini-boss Sergeant Syntax Boss Lord Infinite Loop
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    CHAPTER 2 · PHYSICS
    
        
    Physics Valley
    
        
    Islands float because gravity has been switched off. Use motion, forces, friction and energy to put the laws of nature back where they belong.
    
        
    • Kinematics
    • Newton's laws
    • Friction
    • Work & energy
    
        
    Mini-boss Baron Von Friction Boss The Lord of Broken Laws
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    CHAPTER 3 · CHEMISTRY
    
        
    Chemical Chaos
    
        
    Bubbling beaker towers and an unstable Grand Reactor. Balance equations, build bonds atom by atom and stop the chain reaction before it goes critical.
    
        
    • Atomic structure
    • Bonding
    • Mole concept
    • Reactions
    
        
    Mini-boss Count Catalyst Boss The Lord of the Lab
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    CHAPTER 4 · BIOLOGY
    
        
    Bio-Zone
    
        
    Shrink down and ride the bloodstream. Trace the flow through the heart, fight infections cell by cell, and find out what's corrupting the body from inside.
    
        
    • Cells
    • Circulation
    • Digestion
    • Immunity
    
        
    Mini-boss Professor Plaque Boss The Lord Within
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    CHAPTER 5 · EVERYTHING
    
        
    The Core
    
        
    A broken mix of all four worlds, swapping around you without warning. Every subject at once, against the thing that started it all.
    
        
    • Mixed questions
    • Region swaps
    
        
    Boss The Source
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    THE FINAL EXAM
    
        
    The Exam Hall
    
        
    Rows of desks. A clock that won't stop ticking. Papers in the air. Everything you learned, against the final boss.
    
        
    • Every chapter
    • No hints
    
        
    Final boss Kanha the Dard
